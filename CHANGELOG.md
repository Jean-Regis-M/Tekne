# Changelog

All work was done during the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 window. History is in `git log`.

## 0.1.0 (in progress)

- GPU marbling surface: area-conserving drops, fingertip stylus and multi-tine comb (after Lu, Jaffer et al. 2012)
- Tray snaps to a detected table; paint pots; pinch-and-release drops
- Paper lift-off into framed prints, kept on the device
- Wall gallery: prints hang on the wall you face, avoiding detected windows, doors and wall art
- Wordless five-step first-run lesson with ghost-hand demonstrations
- Procedural spatial audio; pause/resume on blur
- Comfort tiles: hover painting, high contrast, sound; stylus tremor smoothing
- In-headset performance readout (`?hud=1`)
